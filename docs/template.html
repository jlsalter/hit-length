Every Hot 100 hit since 1958, by how long it runs

This goes with my project on whether streaming made songs shorter. Each dot is one of the 24,000+ songs that made the Billboard Hot 100 between 1958 and 2021, placed by the year it first charted and its length on Spotify. The line through the middle is the average for each year. Search for an artist or a song to find it, or hover over any dot.

Data: weekly Billboard Hot 100 charts and Spotify song lengths (TidyTuesday, 2021), and the Year-End Hot 100 with Spotify lengths for 2010–2023. Lengths are the Spotify version of each song; about 1 in 6 songs before 2020 doesn't have one, and 2020–21 are incomplete. Genres come from Spotify's tags for each artist.
